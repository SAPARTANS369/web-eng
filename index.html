What file types can I use to train my AI agent?

You can upload PDFs, TXT, DOCX documents, sync raw text, or provide website URLs for automatic scraping.

How long does it take to set up?

Setup typically takes under 5 minutes. Import your sources, customize your agent's persona, and copy the embed code into your site.

Can the agent capture customer leads?

Yes, you can configure lead capture prompts to collect emails and contact information whenever a customer requests human follow-up.
